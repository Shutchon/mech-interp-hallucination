\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief,}

I am submitting the enclosed manuscript, ``Locating and Mitigating
Factual Hallucination Circuits in Small Language Models via Activation
Patching,'' for consideration as a Regular Paper in the IEICE
Transactions on Information and Systems.

This work presents a systematic activation-patching study of
context--memory conflict in three small language models: Gemma-2-2B,
Llama-3.2-1B, and Qwen2.5-1.5B. The main contributions are:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Causal localization.} A coarse-to-fine patching map
shows that restoration effects concentrate in late-layer attention
outputs at the answer position, replicating across all three
architectures (Spearman 0.81--1.00).

\item \textbf{Head characterization.} Bidirectional patching with a
neutral-control analysis identifies a single specialist head
(L22.H4) whose neutral effect is minimal in both bfloat16 and
float32, and separates it from dual-role heads and from a
bfloat16-selected exploratory set.

\item \textbf{Quantified intervention trade-offs.} Single-head
knockout of the specialist reduces distractor-following by 35\%
while increasing first-token accuracy by 5.3 points, at MMLU
retention 0.992 and a 5\% perplexity cost. All results are
evaluated on held-out relations, with fact-clustered bootstrap CIs
and exact McNemar tests.

\item \textbf{Transparent baselines.} Eight of ten random
single-head knockouts outperform the specialist behaviorally at
comparable capability cost; two carry severe metric-specific costs.
The manuscript reports this openly rather than claiming superiority
of the selection procedure.
\end{enumerate}

All experimental data, analysis scripts, and per-item records are
publicly available on OSF (DOI: 10.17605/OSF.IO/53MYH). The
supplementary material accompanying this submission contains the
complete dataset, head lists, split assignments, and environment
details for reproduction.

I confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published
previously, and is not under consideration by any other journal. I
have no conflicts of interest to disclose.

Thank you for your consideration.
